# Arbiter House

Arbiter House is a native iPhone and iPad app that helps you find language models suited to your prompt. Enter a task, compare models by their documented strengths, and revisit your latest 100 scored prompts in local history.

The app includes a resizable prompt editor, ranked matches with model logos and IDs, retryable partial results, and settings to clear local data. Its interface uses warm parchment colors and a prismatic horizon.

Scoring uses Jev through the free typesafe.pro gateway and requires an internet connection. Your prompt excerpt and model descriptions are sent to typesafe.pro and TypeSafe. Saved history is available offline. Match scores estimate suitability from a dated model catalog; they are not benchmarks or guarantees. The app recommends models and does not run your prompt on them.

This repository hosts public documentation and support. Application source code is maintained separately.

## Support

For questions, bug reports, or suggestions, [open an issue](https://github.com/chigwell/Arbiter-House/issues). See the [support guide](SUPPORT.md) for what to include. Issues are public: do not include private prompts or sensitive information.

## Project status

The app is under development and has not been released on the App Store. A privacy policy covering remote scoring will be published here before public distribution. Clearing local data removes drafts and history from the device; it cannot retract requests already sent to the scoring service.
